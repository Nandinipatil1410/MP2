TITLE: Data Processing Agreement Between Aurora Technologies and NovaTech Inc.
============================================================

This Data Processing Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into on February 10, 2023, by and between Aurora Technologies, with its principal place of business at 123 Main Street, Suite 400, New York, NY 10001, and represented by its CEO, Emily J. Lee (SSN: 123-45-6789, DOB: 08/12/1985), and NovaTech Inc., with its principal place of business at 456 Elm Street, Suite 200, Chicago, IL 60611, and represented by its CTO, David M. Kim (SSN: 987-65-4321, DOB: 02/25/1978).

1. Purpose
The purpose of this Agreement is to outline the terms and conditions under which NovaTech Inc. will process personal data on behalf of Aurora Technologies, as part of their contractual agreement valued at $250,000.00, with a processing fee of $0.05 per record, and an estimated 500,000 records to be processed over the term of the agreement, resulting in total processing fees of $25,000.00.

2. Definitions
For the purposes of this Agreement, the following definitions shall apply: "Personal Data" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, such as names, addresses, phone numbers, and email addresses. "Processing" means any operation or set of operations which is performed on Personal Data, such as collection, recording, organization, storage, adaptation or alteration, retrieval, consultation, use, disclosure by transmission, dissemination or otherwise making available, alignment or combination, restriction, erasure or destruction.

3. Obligations
NovaTech Inc. shall process Personal Data only in accordance with the instructions of Aurora Technologies, as set forth in this Agreement. NovaTech Inc. shall ensure that its employees, agents, and contractors who have access to Personal Data have committed to confidentiality and have received appropriate training on data protection. The total number of personnel involved in the data processing is estimated to be 10, with 2 personnel dedicated to data security and 8 personnel dedicated to data processing, resulting in a personnel cost of $100,000.00 per annum, or $833.33 per month.

4. Data Security
NovaTech Inc. shall implement and maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect Personal Data against unauthorized or unlawful processing, accidental loss, destruction, or damage. The security measures shall include, but not be limited to, encryption, firewalls, and access controls. The estimated cost of implementing these security measures is $15,000.00, with an annual maintenance cost of $5,000.00.

5. Data Breach
In the event of a data breach, NovaTech Inc. shall notify Aurora Technologies without undue delay, and in any event within 24 hours of becoming aware of the breach. The notification shall include, but not be limited to, the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number of data subjects and Personal Data records affected, and the measures taken or proposed to be taken to mitigate the breach. The cost of notification is estimated to be $2,000.00 per breach, with a maximum liability of $10,000.00.

TITLE: Data Processing Agreement Between NovaTech Inc. and QuickData Services
============================================================

This Data Processing Agreement (the "Agreement") is made and entered into on February 10, 2024, by and between NovaTech Inc., with its principal place of business at 123 Main Street, Suite 456, New York, NY 10001, and QuickData Services, with its principal place of business at 789 Park Avenue, Suite 101, Los Angeles, CA 90001.

WHEREAS, NovaTech Inc. (the "Data Controller") has appointed QuickData Services (the "Data Processor") to process certain personal data on its behalf, as more fully described in Appendix A (the "Data Processing Schedule").

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, the parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions. For the purposes of this Agreement, the following definitions shall apply:
(a) "Personal Data" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person (a "Data Subject");
(b) "Data Subject" means an individual who is the subject of Personal Data, including but not limited to, employees, customers, and contractors of the Data Controller.

2. Scope of Processing. QuickData Services shall process Personal Data only to the extent necessary to perform its obligations under this Agreement and in accordance with the instructions of the Data Controller, as set forth in the Data Processing Schedule.

3. Data Protection Requirements. QuickData Services shall implement and maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect Personal Data against unauthorized or unlawful processing, accidental loss, destruction, or damage, including but not limited to:
(a) encrypting Personal Data in transit and at rest;
(b) implementing access controls to ensure that only authorized personnel have access to Personal Data;
(c) conducting regular security audits and risk assessments.

The cost of implementing these measures is estimated to be $150,000, which will be borne by QuickData Services. Additionally, QuickData Services will provide training to its personnel on data protection at a cost of $20 per employee, with a total of 50 employees requiring training, resulting in a total training cost of $1,000.

4. Data Subject Rights. The Data Controller shall be responsible for responding to requests from Data Subjects to exercise their rights under applicable data protection laws, including but not limited to the right to access, rectify, and erase their Personal Data. QuickData Services shall cooperate with the Data Controller in responding to such requests and shall provide the Data Controller with any information necessary to respond to such requests.

5. Term and Termination. This Agreement shall commence on the date first above written and shall continue for a term of 24 months. Upon expiration or termination of this Agreement, QuickData Services shall return or destroy all Personal Data in its possession, unless otherwise agreed upon by the parties in writing.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the date first above written.

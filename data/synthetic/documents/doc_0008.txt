Pre-Operative Diagnosis: Cholelithiasis

Intraoperative Findings: The patient was taken to the operating room and placed under general anesthesia. A standard cholecystectomy was performed with a 4-port approach. The cystic duct was clipped and divided, and the cystic artery was ligated with 4-0 Vicryl suture. The gallbladder was dissected from the liver bed, and the liver was noted to have mild fatty infiltration.

Operative Time: 67 minutes
Estimated Blood Loss (EBL): 150 mL
Urine Output: 300 mL

Post-Operative Plan: The patient was transferred to the recovery room in stable condition. She will be started on a clear liquid diet and advanced to a regular diet as tolerated. Pain management will be with IV acetaminophen and oral oxycodone as needed. The patient will be discharged home on post-operative day 1 with a 7-day course of cephalexin 500 mg PO BID for prophylaxis against wound infection.

Complications: None noted during the operation. However, the patient did experience a 10 mmHg drop in systolic blood pressure during the dissection of the gallbladder from the liver bed. This was managed with a bolus of 500 mL of lactated Ringer's solution.

Pathology: The gallbladder was sent to pathology for examination. Preliminary results indicate the presence of multiple small gallstones, with the largest measuring 6 mm in diameter. The final pathology report is pending.

Discharge Medications:
- Acetaminophen 650 mg PO Q4H as needed for pain
- Oxycodone 5 mg PO Q4H as needed for pain
- Cephalexin 500 mg PO BID for 7 days

Follow-Up: The patient will follow up in the clinic in 2 weeks for removal of staples and wound inspection.

EMPLOYMENT CONTRACT

This Employment Contract ("Contract") is made and entered into on this 25th day of February, 2024 ("Effective Date"), by and between:

XYZ Corporation ("Company"), a corporation with its principal place of business at 123 Main Street, New York, NY 10001, and

John Doe ("Employee"), an individual with a social security number of 123-45-6789, born on January 1, 1990, and residing at 456 Elm Street, New York, NY 10002.

WHEREAS, the Company desires to employ the Employee as a Software Engineer, and the Employee desires to accept such employment with the Company.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and promises contained herein, the parties agree as follows:

1. TERM. The term of this Contract shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for a period of two (2) years, unless terminated earlier in accordance with Section 8.

2. COMPENSATION. The Company shall pay the Employee a salary of $120,000 per year, paid bi-weekly in the amount of $4,615.38, consisting of 26 pay periods. The Employee shall also be eligible for a bonus of up to 15% of their annual salary, based on performance, which shall be paid on or before December 31st of each year. Additionally, the Employee shall receive 10% of their annual salary as a signing bonus, which shall be paid on the first pay period after the Effective Date. The Employee shall also receive reimbursement for business expenses, up to a maximum of $5,000 per year, based on a reimbursement rate of $0.50 per mile for business use of their personal vehicle.

3. BENEFITS. The Company shall provide the Employee with health, dental, and vision insurance, with a premium cost of $500 per month, of which the Company shall pay 80%, and the Employee shall pay 20%, which is $100 per month.

4. VACATION. The Employee shall be entitled to 15 days of paid vacation per year, which shall accrue at a rate of 1.25 days per month.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY. The Employee shall maintain the confidentiality of all Company information and shall not disclose such information to any third party without the prior written consent of the Company.

6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY. The Employee agrees to assign to the Company all rights, title, and interest in and to any inventions, discoveries, or improvements made by the Employee during the term of this Contract.

7. NON-COMPETITION. The Employee agrees not to engage in any activity that competes with the business of the Company during the term of this Contract and for a period of one (1) year after termination.

8. TERMINATION. This Contract may be terminated by either party upon 30 days written notice to the other party. In the event of termination, the Employee shall be entitled to receive any accrued but unpaid salary, bonus, and benefits.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Contract as of the date first above written.

Subject: Fiscal Analysis of the Accounts Receivable Department

Introduction:
This report presents the findings from the internal audit conducted on the Accounts Receivable (AR) department for the Q2 2023 period. The audit aimed to review the efficiency, effectiveness, and compliance of the AR processes.

Key Findings:
1. Accounts Receivable Turnover Ratio: 6.25
2. Days Sales Outstanding: 45 days
3. Accounts Receivable as a percentage of Sales: 22%

Analysis:
The Accounts Receivable Turnover Ratio indicates that the department collects revenue 6.25 times the average accounts receivable balance within a year. This is a moderate performance, with room for improvement to increase the frequency of collections.

The Days Sales Outstanding shows that customers on average take 45 days to pay invoices. This is above the industry standard of 30-45 days, suggesting potential delays in cash flow.

The Accounts Receivable as a percentage of Sales reveals that a significant portion of the company's revenue is tied up in uncollected receivables. This may affect liquidity and should be monitored closely.

Conclusion:
The audit highlights areas for improvement in the Accounts Receivable department, particularly in speeding up collections and reducing Days Sales Outstanding. Recommendations will be provided in a follow-up report.
